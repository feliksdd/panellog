## Remaining work

### 39 `lib_footprint_mismatch` warnings

The board's 39 footprint *instances* differ from their linked KiCad/PanelLog library copies. Most were generated in the placement draft and use approximated artwork/courtyard/pad and assembly information. **Do not disable this check to pass DRC.** For standard, manufacturer-qualified resistor/capacitor/diode/SOT-23/crystal footprints, use KiCad 10 PCB editor → **Tools → Update Footprints from Library…** selectively, reviewing rotations, pads and existing net assignments. For project-specific `*_DRAFT` footprints (BVB Kelvin, Infineon TOLL, E73, LM5164 DDA, provisional L1/MC4), first validate the manufacturer's physical land-pattern, paste, mask, pad numbering and RF/mechanical keepouts; only then synchronize the schematic and board.

### 131 unconnected items

The board is *almost entirely unrouted*, so these messages are expected but represent real electrical incompleteness. The 9-mm PV+ and 5-mm SHUNT_RETURN study segments have not been validated for 20 A, no full low-side return or Kelvin routing exists, and there is no valid RF copper keepout. Complete the placement/footprints and current paths before attempting other routing or Gerber fabrication.
