# PanelLog – horizontal power path and two-sided copper, 2026-11-04

The interrupted layout edit is complete. Q1 is rotated to -90 degrees, with source on the left and drain on the right. Both negative pigtail connections, Q1's power-pad centres and the shunt force pads follow y = 84 mm. The positive VDC path remains horizontal at y = 66 mm.

## Copper and routing

- GND planes on F.Cu and B.Cu, respecting the existing mechanical and antenna keepouts.
- VDC, GND source return, SHUNT_RETURN and MPPT_MINUS have paired top/bottom copper areas. Vias connect only matching nets; no stitching crosses the shunt or Q1 electrically.
- Power-path stitching uses 0.8 mm lands / 0.4 mm drills, with nominal 2 mm grid where clearance permits. Additional ground-plane stitching uses a nominal 6 mm grid. Vias are excluded from component pad envelopes and connector drill regions.
- Total vias on these nets, including retained original vias: GND 165, VDC 97, SHUNT_RETURN 22, MPPT_MINUS 61.
- Q1 gate resistor R9 repositioned and gate/control traces rerouted. RT2 moved below Q1 and its signal routing reconnected; its ground pad joins the ground plane.
- Shunt sense pads retain independent SHUNT_SENSE_P / SHUNT_SENSE_M traces. Force copper is not used as a Kelvin trace.
