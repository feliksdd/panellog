## Non-purchased PCB features and external assembly

J1 is absent from the current design and marked removed. Current power pads
are J2/J6 (VDC), J5 (MPPT_MINUS), J7 (GND). J3 is the sensor cable solder pad;
J4 consists of programming pogo lands. H1–H4 are drilled mounting holes.
They have no separate LCSC order number and zero purchased-component quantity.
Do not buy four fictitious connector bodies from these PCB references.

External PV leads, compatible connector pairs, sensor cable/encapsulation,
cable glands, enclosure and spacers remain mechanically unspecified. No
arbitrary LCSC part is assigned without wire size, cable length, mating system
and enclosure interface. Their cost is **not included and not finalized**.
C16/C17/Y1 are removed; C23/D1/R23 are DNP and excluded. DNP snubber/TVS values
must follow transient qualification rather than being selected to fill a BOM cell.
