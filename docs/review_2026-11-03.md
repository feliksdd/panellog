# PanelLog – KiCad 10 review and routed prototype, 2026-11-03

Status: routed prototype, NOT production-released. This report supersedes earlier statements that this revision has not been run in KiCad. Native KiCad CLI and pcbnew 10.0.8 were used.

## Changes

- BVB shunt: manufacturer land pattern, independent force pins 1/2 and sense pins 3/4. Separate schematic nets preserve Kelvin routing.
- LM5164: DDA0008B lands and separate grounded exposed pad 9; divided paste apertures and thermal vias.
- IPT015N10N5: OEM TOLL lands, gate 1, source 2–8, drain 9.
- E73: 43-pad geometry and antenna keepout on both copper layers.
- C3: polarized schematic symbol and Panasonic F 8 x 10.2 mm footprint.
- L1: Würth 7447709680 (68 µH, 120 V) replaces the 30 V SXN candidate. BOM updated; no LCSC availability claimed.
- Placement regrouped for buck input loop, current sensing, and radio antenna. Power and Kelvin connections routed explicitly; remaining signals completed through Specctra routing import.
- Ground plane added on B.Cu. Drain thermal spreading remains electrically separate. Net-name escaping and datasheet fields synchronized with schematic.

The reports explicitly list inherited disabled checks (including missing courtyard, footprint filters, and component-type mismatch). No new violation exclusions were added to achieve these results. DRC is against existing project rules; it is not a certification of all manufacturing or electrical requirements.

Front and rear copper exports were generated for review. High-current routing is 9 mm on the positive pass-through, 8 mm on the source return bus and 4.5 mm on the shunt/output return, with narrower local source-pad fanout. Design target is 70 µm copper; the complete 20 A temperature rise including pad necks, solder joints and cable terminations is not yet verified. This remains a prototype limitation, not a current rating approval.

## Outstanding before manufacturing release

- Confirm fabricator stackup/finished copper and via-in-pad filling/capping for U1; otherwise revise the thermal via arrangement before ordering.
- Validate 20 A heating of Q1, shunt, power paths and pigtail joints at maximum enclosure temperature. Check mechanical cable strain relief and actual pigtail fit.
- Verify effective MLCC capacitance at voltage/temperature, exact MPNs still missing in BOM, electrolytic lifetime, PV surge margin and gate-bias behavior.
- Bench-check buck startup/load steps, switching overshoot and optional snubber values; calibrate current/voltage/temperature channels.
- Verify RF operation in the intended enclosure. Clean ERC/DRC does not establish electrical performance or EMC compliance.
