# L1: Sumida CDRH127/LDNP-680MC (C2452974)

Replaces Würth 7447709680 at the existing position. Inductance stays 68 µH
±20%. Manufacturer DCR is 92 mΩ typical, 120 mΩ maximum, rather than treating
the catalogue's 92 mΩ as a maximum. At 0.5 A this means 23–30 mW winding loss
at room temperature, excluding ripple and core losses. The 2.6 A saturation
specification provides substantial margin over LM5163's 0.87 A maximum listed
peak-current threshold (switching-delay overshoot still needs qualification).

The Sumida temperature ceiling is 100 °C including self-heating. It must be
checked in the hot enclosure; it is lower than the former Würth rating.
No unsupported 120 V component rating is carried over to Sumida.

The recommended lands, rotated 90 degrees, are 2.9 × 5.4 mm with 7 mm gap.
Their positions match the previous footprint, retaining routing. A top-layer
7 × 12.3 mm central keepout excludes tracks, vias and copper fill. Two GND
stitching vias were removed from that area. The footprint has two electrical
pads spanning the paired physical contacts. The incorrect Würth STEP model
was removed; an exact Sumida STEP is not available in this change.
